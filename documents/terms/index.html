NUMUW - Commercial Terms

مسودة مرجع تجاري تنظّم النطاق والتغييرات والملكية والدفع والتسليم، وتحتاج مراجعة قبل اعتماد عقد نهائي.

Commercial controls

  • Scope: deliverables, exclusions, timeline and acceptance point are written before work starts.
  • Change control: new requirements are evaluated and re-estimated before implementation; approved changes update scope, timing and price.
  • Payment: milestones, due dates, taxes/fees and any deposits are stated explicitly in the signed proposal or agreement.
  • Third parties: domains, hosting, software, ads and external services are identified separately and are not assumed included.
  • Ownership: client ownership and any licensed/third-party assets are documented; transfer follows the signed scope and applicable payment terms.
  • Acceptance: delivery criteria, review window and unresolved change requests are recorded before final handover.
  • Support: support window, response expectations and out-of-scope work are stated in the agreement.
  • Results: no guaranteed rankings, sales, ROI, savings or financial outcomes are promised.
  • Confidentiality / liability / jurisdiction: must be defined in the final agreement according to the actual engagement and professional legal advice.
هذه صفحة تشغيلية وليست عقدًا أو رأيًا قانونيًا. النسخة النهائية للاستخدام التجاري يجب أن تطابق الحقائق والعرض والعقد الفعلي.
