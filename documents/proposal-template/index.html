Core contents

  • Client context and current state
  • Business objective and success measure
  • Recommended system and rationale
  • Deliverables and explicit exclusions
  • Dependencies and client responsibilities

  • Investment, taxes/fees and payment milestones
  • Revision / change-request rules
  • Ownership, licensing and asset handover
  • Acceptance criteria and sign-off
  • Support window and out-of-scope work
  • Proposal validity / expiry date
