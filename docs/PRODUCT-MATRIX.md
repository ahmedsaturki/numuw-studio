# NUMUW Product Matrix

| Order | Product | Role |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | NUMUW Diagnostic | Paid decision work: clarify the problem, current state, priorities, risks and next intervention |
| 2 | Digital Kickoff | Focused digital foundation for an already-clear need |
| 3 | Automation Sprint | One clearly defined workflow from mapping through test and handover |
| 4 | Growth System | Connected work across multiple growth/operations layers |
| 5 | Growth Partner | Recurring optimization after a useful baseline and owned assets exist |

Every product must have:
- a clear audience and problem
- written scope
- deliverables
- acceptance criteria
- ownership terms
- dependencies and exclusions
- a commercial next step

Free Fit Conversation is a separate pre-commercial qualification path and is not a substitute name for the Diagnostic product.
