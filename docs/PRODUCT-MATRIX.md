# NUMUW Product Matrix

The five offers are one commercial ladder. The homepage, product hub, product pages and routing tools must use the same names, roles, reference prices and decision logic.

| Product | Best fit | Primary job | Reference | Acceptance anchor |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| NUMUW Diagnostic | Problem or priority is unclear, or the cost of choosing wrongly is high | Discover, baseline, map gaps, prioritize | Free fit call; deeper diagnostic by written scope | Clear priority, evidence gaps and next scope |
| Digital Kickoff | Focused digital foundation is enough | Establish a credible owned starting asset | From 7,900 EGP | Agreed page/search/brand starter works and is handed over |
| Automation Sprint | One repeatable process is clear | Turn one process into a testable workflow | From 8,000 EGP | Agreed workflow passes critical tests and is documented |
| Growth System | Multiple dependent growth layers matter | Connect strategy, presence, conversion, one workflow and measurement | From 24,900 EGP | Agreed layers work, dependencies are documented and baseline exists |
| Growth Partner | Continuous optimization is the operating need | Run a recurring priority, execution, measurement and decision loop | From 6,500 EGP / month | Each cycle has an accepted output and a recorded next decision |

## Product contract

Every product page, proposal and sales conversation should make these explicit:

- Audience and fit
- Non-fit conditions
- Deliverables
- Explicit exclusions
- Dependencies and client responsibilities
- Timeline or cadence
- Reference price or scoped price
- Acceptance criteria
- Ownership and licensing
- Support boundary
- Next action

The public site must not create a second product name or parallel price ladder without updating this matrix, the homepage, product hub and routing tools.

## Routing rule

Unclear problem → Diagnostic → smallest valid intervention → broader system only when evidence justifies it → Partner when continuous optimization becomes the operating model.

This is a decision architecture, not an upsell ladder.
