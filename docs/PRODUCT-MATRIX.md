# NUMUW Product Matrix

The product catalog is the commercial source of truth. Capabilities and landing pages route into these products instead of acting as disconnected offers.

| Product | Primary job | Buyer condition | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| NUMUW Diagnostic | Reduce decision risk | Problem / priority / scope is unclear | Scope-based |
| Digital Kickoff | Establish a credible owned digital foundation | Presence is the main gap and scope can stay focused | From 7,900 EGP |
| Automation Sprint | Fix one recurring workflow | Process is clear, repeated and measurable | From 8,000 EGP |
| Growth System | Connect multiple growth layers | Message, conversion, operations and measurement need one system | From 24,900 EGP |
| Growth Partner | Compound improvement | A stable foundation exists and continuous ownership is useful | From 6,500 EGP / month |

## Product contract

Every product page must explain:

1. Who it is for.
2. The problem it is designed to address.
3. When it is not the right starting point.
4. Specific deliverables.
5. Dependencies and client responsibilities.
6. Acceptance criteria.
7. Ownership / licensing boundary.
8. Support boundary.
9. Price reference or explicit scope-based pricing.
10. The next action.

## Offer architecture

Use:

**Capability → Solution page → Decision tool / Diagnostic → Product → Discovery → Scope → Delivery**

Do not use:

**Service list → generic CTA → undefined proposal**

## Pricing rule

Homepage references and product-page references must match the same catalog. A price is a starting point unless the scope says otherwise. Final scope, deliverables, timeline and payment terms are written before execution.

## Product vs capability

Capabilities are implementation ingredients:

- strategy
- brand / identity
- websites
- SEO
- automation
- AI-assisted workflows
- CRM / measurement
- conversion optimization

They are not automatically separate products.

## Proof rule

Each product needs evidence appropriate to its maturity:
- real case study when available
- public demonstration when possible
- method / artifact / acceptance evidence when a client case cannot be published

Never manufacture outcomes.
