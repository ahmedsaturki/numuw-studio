# NUMUW Brand Readiness Gate

**Status: HOLD major irreversible brand spend pending clearance.**

This is a commercial risk gate, not a legal conclusion that the name cannot be used.

## Why this gate exists

Current web research shows multiple active brands using the same or closely related NUMUW / NUMU / Numu name family in adjacent markets and categories, including a Bahrain-based Numuw child/family telehealth platform and multiple UAE marketing/creative businesses using Numu.

That creates potential risk in:
- search disambiguation and branded discovery
- direct-navigation collisions
- social handles and marketplace naming
- future regional expansion
- trademark/service-class clearance
- customer confusion between unrelated businesses

## Before major spend

Before committing heavily to trademark filing, large paid acquisition, extensive printed collateral, major partner distribution or long-term brand architecture, complete a documented clearance review covering:
- Egypt trademark databases and applicable classes
- WIPO / Madrid resources where relevant
- target-market trademark databases
- domain portfolio
- social handles
- app stores / marketplaces
- branded search collision
- Arabic and Latin spelling variants
- closely related service categories

## Current posture

Use `NUMUW | نُمو` consistently for the current product experiment while treating it as a **working master brand pending clearance**.

Do not claim trademark ownership or exclusivity.

Do not create multiple sub-brands merely to hide a master-brand clearance problem.

## Decision owner

The final legal/name decision belongs to the business owner after appropriate professional clearance. This document exists to prevent silent exposure of the risk.

## Reassessment triggers

Re-run the gate before:
- major paid distribution
- trademark commitment
- regional expansion
- external partnership distribution
- irreversible packaging / physical production
