# NUMUW Brand Readiness Gate

## Status
Not cleared for irreversible brand investment yet.
This is an internal decision gate, not a claim that the name cannot be used.

## Why this matters
Exact and near-exact variants of the NUMUW / NUMU / Numu name family are already used by active businesses and products in adjacent markets and regions.
Examples include the Bahrain-based Numuw child/family telehealth platform and UAE-based Numu marketing/creative businesses.
Risks include search ambiguity, direct-navigation collisions, social-handle availability, marketplace/app confusion, legal clearance cost, and expansion into markets where a similar mark already exists.

## Decision gate before major spend
Before trademark filing, packaging, large paid campaigns, major domain strategy, printed collateral, partner distribution or long-term brand architecture, run a formal clearance review covering Egypt trademark databases, WIPO/Madrid resources where relevant, target-market trademark databases, domains, social handles, app stores, search collisions, service classes, and confusing Arabic/Latin spellings.

## Current practical posture
Use NUMUW | نُمو consistently in the current product experiment, but treat the name as a working brand pending clearance.
Do not make legal exclusivity claims.
Do not create sub-brands merely to compensate for an uncleared master brand.

## Reassessment trigger
Revisit this gate before major paid distribution, formal trademark commitment, or expansion outside the current Egyptian B2B / growth-systems positioning.
