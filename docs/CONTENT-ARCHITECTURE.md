# NUMUW Content Architecture

## Content moat

NUMUW should publish material that remains useful even when the reader never buys:
- decision guides
- teardown / diagnostic analyses
- checklists
- operating playbooks
- original models
- real case evidence

Google's current guidance emphasizes helpful, reliable, people-first content and warns against producing lots of search-first content with little original value. Source: Google Search Central.

## Content jobs

### Acquisition
Answer a real buyer question and expose relevant expertise.

### Decision support
Help a buyer understand whether the issue is positioning, conversion, demand, operations, measurement or ongoing optimization.

### Trust
Show methods, proof standards, ownership and boundaries.

### Conversion
Move the visitor to the smallest reasonable next step.

## Editorial lifecycle

Idea → source research → original angle → draft → fact check → commercial/legal review → publish → observe → update/retire.

## Proof language

Every substantive claim should be classed as:
- observed
- measured
- benchmark
- scenario
- hypothesis

Avoid anonymous “results” language without source and context.
