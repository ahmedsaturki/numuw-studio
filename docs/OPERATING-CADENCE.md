# NUMUW Operating Cadence

## Every change

Classify the change as Bug, UX/accessibility, Content, SEO, Commercial, Security/privacy, Performance or Research.

Record:
- affected routes/files
- reason
- expected behavior
- verification performed
- remaining external checks

## Before publishing

Run:

```bash
node scripts/release-gate.mjs
```

Then review the changed pages visually on desktop and mobile in a real browser.

## Weekly operating review

- Broken links / route inventory
- CTA destinations
- pricing and offer consistency
- new proof or case evidence
- open release-gate issue
- security/reporting notices
- upcoming content that would create duplicate pages

## Monthly commercial review

Review:
- pricing and margins
- product fit/non-fit
- conversion paths
- qualified conversations
- proposal acceptance
- delivery friction
- handover completeness

Do not optimize traffic in isolation from qualified commercial outcomes.

## Quarterly system review

Re-evaluate:
- ICP and specialist verticals
- products that are actually being bought
- pages with low decision value
- evidence/case-study depth
- performance and accessibility
- privacy/security posture
- third-party dependencies
- archive/merge candidates

## Content publishing rule

A new article, landing page or tool must have a distinct decision job and original value. Do not create near-duplicate routes for keywords or industries without a genuinely different buyer problem.
