# NUMUW Proof Acquisition Playbook

Status: Active operating contract

## Purpose
Turn real delivery work into sourceable commercial evidence without inventing clients, results, testimonials, logos or performance claims.

The proof system separates:

**Description → Sourceable Artifact → Measured Result → Permissioned Case Study**

A page may describe what NUMUW does without evidence. A result claim requires an identifiable source, context and limits. A published customer case additionally requires appropriate publication permission.

## Delivery loop

### 1. Instrument the engagement
Before execution, agree what can be observed or measured, who owns the baseline, the relevant system of record and the evidence that can safely be captured.

### 2. Capture the baseline
Record the current state before the intervention, including the measurement definition, date or time window, source and relevant assumptions.

### 3. Capture artifacts
Preserve useful evidence during delivery:

- approved designs or page versions
- test outputs
- deployment records
- workflow results
- measurement exports
- links and screenshots with context
- acceptance and handover records

A screenshot without a source, period and context is not sufficient evidence for a material result claim.

### 4. Validate the result
Compare the result with the agreed baseline and scope. Check whether other changes, missing data, attribution limits or external factors could explain the observed difference.

Do not convert activity counts into business outcomes without a defensible relationship.

### 5. Request publication permission
Record what may be published:

- client/company name or anonymized treatment
- logo
- testimonial
- metrics
- screenshots
- URLs
- timeframe
- quoted statement
- restrictions or expiry

No permission means no public case-study claim.

### 6. Publish
Use the case structure:

**Context → Baseline → Problem → Intervention → Evidence → Result → Limits → Learning**

Separate the sourceable fact from interpretation. Keep the measurement window and important caveats visible.

### 7. Review
Re-check published cases when:

- a source is replaced or removed
- permission changes or expires
- a metric is revised
- a published URL changes
- a material claim is no longer supportable

Update or remove the public claim rather than preserving stale proof.

## Minimum case record
Every candidate case should have:

- context
- baseline
- problem
- intervention
- evidence_source
- result
- limits
- learning
- publication_permission

These fields are represented in data/numuw-system.json and are part of the system contract.

## Evidence examples

### Technical / product work
Prefer reproducible test output, deployment evidence, versioned artifacts, acceptance records or independently checkable URLs.

### Conversion / marketing work
Use a measurement source with a defined timeframe and baseline. Distinguish traffic, engagement, leads, qualified opportunities, revenue and other stages rather than treating them as interchangeable.

### Operations / automation
Use workflow samples, timestamps, process measures, error rates, throughput or other agreed operational indicators with a clear measurement definition.

### Content / SEO
Use sourceable pages, documented changes and measurement windows. Do not claim rankings, traffic or indexing gains without a verifiable source.

## Discovery and onboarding requirements
Before delivery starts, identify:

- the business problem and expected decision
- the baseline owner
- the evidence owner
- the measurement source
- the relevant access
- the intended publication permission level
- any confidentiality or anonymization rules

If measurement is not appropriate, document that explicitly and avoid creating a fake quantitative success story later.

## Publication gate
A public result claim is releasable only when:

1. the intervention is inside the agreed scope;
2. the evidence source is identifiable;
3. the measurement window is clear;
4. relevant limits or attribution issues are stated;
5. publication permission exists at the required level;
6. the published copy matches the evidence.

## Current NUMUW posture
The public NUMUW site currently has **no published client case evidence**. That is an intentional truth boundary, not a missing-data excuse.

Until real cases are available, NUMUW should build trust through:

- accurate descriptions of capabilities
- proof-of-work from NUMUW-owned artifacts
- reproducible technical evidence
- transparent product and delivery contracts
- honest learning and methodology
- permissioned client evidence when it becomes available

## Exit rule
When a claim cannot be tied to a source, context and appropriate permission, do not publish the claim as a result.

Publish the process, artifact, limitation or learning instead.
