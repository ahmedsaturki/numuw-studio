|---|---|---|---:|---|---|

## Productization contract

Every offer page must state:
- fit
- non-fit
- scope
- exclusions
- inputs/dependencies
- time/cadence
- pricing basis
- acceptance
- ownership
- support
- next action

## Pricing truth

Published anchors are reference starting points, not blanket quotes. The signed scope or proposal is the commercial authority.

Third-party fees, ad spend, software, hosting and external services are separate unless explicitly included.

## Delivery principle

Use fixed appetite/time boxes and controlled scope. Quality must not be traded away to preserve an arbitrary feature list. Scope may shrink to protect completion and acceptance.
