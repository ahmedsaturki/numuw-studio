# Competitive Benchmark and Learning Log

Date: 2026-10-04

## Refine Labs
- explicit ICP and business context
- category-level narrative instead of a generic service list
- proof is a central sales surface
- case studies are structured around problem, work and measured result
- current positioning connects Brand, Demand and Expand instead of isolated tactics

NUMUW lesson: make the buyer and trigger explicit, lead with the bottleneck, and build a real evidence library over time.

## KlientBoost
- outcome and accountability are prominent
- discovery considers financial, marketing, customer-success, competitor and market inputs
- a plan/assessment is used as a strong entry point

NUMUW lesson: make diagnosis and business inputs more visible before asking a visitor to choose an implementation service.

## Velocity Partners
- portfolio is a major trust surface
- capabilities are demonstrated through real engagements
- work spans positioning, identity, content, websites and lead generation

NUMUW lesson: demonstrate breadth through real work rather than a longer service inventory.

## Google Search
- people-first content has a real audience and purpose
- structured data is useful for clarifying meaning but is not a guarantee of rich-result display
- real multilingual editions should use crawlable language-specific URLs and appropriate alternate annotations

## Accessibility and performance
Focus visibility, focus clearance, target size, loading, interaction and layout stability are product qualities, not paperwork.

References:
https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag/
https://web.dev/articles/vitals

## Do not copy blindly
- inflated outcome numbers
- fake trust badges
- generic service menus disconnected from buyer decisions
- large low-value SEO page libraries
- hidden third-party runtime dependencies
- visual complexity that hurts clarity or speed
- treating a scenario calculator as a measured business result

Working principle: borrow proven patterns; never borrow unverified claims.
