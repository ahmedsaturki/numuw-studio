# Content & Proof Policy

NUMUW should compete through clarity and evidence.

Never publish:
- invented client logos
- invented testimonials
- invented awards
- invented rankings
- fabricated revenue/ROI/performance
- guarantees of Google rankings

Use:
- real case studies
- before/after evidence when measurable
- screenshots from real projects
- client-approved testimonials
- dates and scope
- clearly labeled estimates and scenarios

Marketing language should describe what NUMUW does, not claim outcomes that have not been measured.
